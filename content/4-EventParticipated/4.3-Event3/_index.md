### Event Overview
* **Event Name:** Cloud Architect Game Show
* **Date & Time:** June 20, 2026
* **Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City
* **Role:** Attendee

### Core Themes & Highlights

**1. Gamified Learning Experience**
To bring a fresh and engaging atmosphere while maintaining the spirit of learning and sharing, the program organized the "Cloud Architect" game show. Instead of traditional seminars, 8 teams (5 members each) battled head-to-head by answering a series of Cloud computing and architectural design questions ranging from easy to hard. 

**2. Team Formation & Networking**
The event encouraged cross-networking. Teams were not restricted to existing internship groups; we had the freedom to recruit members from different teams to form our ultimate 5-person squad. The only rule was to keep it fair—no seasoned industry experts allowed!

**3. Strategic Gameplay & Lifelines**
The competition wasn't just a test of AWS knowledge; it heavily relied on strategy and risk management. Each team had two special lifelines to use once during the match:
*   **Minimal Risk (Rủi ro tối thiểu):** Used when the team is uncertain. If the answer is wrong, no points are deducted. However, if correct, the team only receives half the points for that question.
*   **Star of Hope (Ngôi sao hi vọng):** Used for the question the team is most confident about. A correct answer doubles the points earned, but a wrong answer results in a double point deduction.

**4. The Thrill of the Tie-Breaker**
The competition format was brutal but fun. The team with the most points advanced to the next round. The highlight of the matches was the intense "Question 11" tie-breaker. Whenever two teams tied at the end of the regular rounds, this sudden-death question tested not only our architectural knowledge but also presented last-minute, game-changing scenarios.

### Personal Reflection
This event was a brilliant way to relieve stress while reinforcing the heavy architectural knowledge we've been absorbing over the past weeks. Discussing AWS services and architectural best practices under the pressure of a ticking clock and competitive opponents forced us to think on our feet. It proved that learning Cloud architecture doesn't always have to be dry; it can be incredibly thrilling when combined with teamwork and a bit of game strategy.
